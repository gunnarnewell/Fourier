How it works

Your drawing is sampled into 768 equally spaced points along its length. We compute complex Fourier coefficients \(C_k\) and animate epicycles following \(\sum_k C_k e^{i2\pi k t}\). Increase Epicycles for more detail.

Tips

  • Use Fit to center/scale your drawing.
  • Try fewer Samples for faster recompute, then increase.
  • "Close path" is best for logos/shapes. Turn it off for open strokes.
  • Import/Export uses normalized coordinates so it scales to any canvas.
